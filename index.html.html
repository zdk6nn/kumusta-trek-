01 / About the film

A birthday.
A return. A reckoning.

On his 21st birthday, Trek, a self-doubting guitarist returns to his hometown after abandoning his band. But as he wanders old haunts with his childhood friend, a simple birthday gift triggers a quiet, surreal reckoning with the fears and insecurities that forced him to walk away from his passion.
